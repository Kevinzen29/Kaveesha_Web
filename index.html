02 — Values

Four principles I use to keep product decisions grounded—from the first idea to the final interaction.

01

Useful

Start with a real need. Make the outcome clear, practical and genuinely helpful.

↗
02

Considered

Think through context, systems, edge cases and what happens beyond the happy path.

↗
03

Beautiful

Use typography, hierarchy, motion and restraint to make experiences feel intentional.

↗
04

Well made

Care about implementation and details. The final product should feel as good as the idea.

↗
How that shows up

I like to think broadly about the product, then zoom all the way into the smallest interaction—connecting user needs, business goals and craft into one coherent experience.
