Case study — Product design

PlayMade

A product experience designed to connect people through sport, activity and shared participation.

StrategyUX/UIProduct
Industry

01 — Overview

Creating a clearer way to discover, organise and join play.

PlayMade brings people and activities together in a product designed around participation. The experience focuses on making opportunities easy to understand and actions easy to take.

02 — Challenge

Reduce the distance between interest and participation.

Users need to quickly understand what is happening, where it is, who it is for and how to join. The design challenge was surfacing that information without making discovery feel transactional.

03 — Approach

People first.
Participation next.

Journeys were shaped around natural questions users ask when deciding whether to take part. Clear information architecture and focused calls to action keep the experience moving.

A product language built around community.
