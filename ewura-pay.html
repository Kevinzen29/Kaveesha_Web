Case study — Product design

Ewura Pay

A modern payment experience focused on trust, speed and everyday financial clarity.

StrategyUX/UIProduct
Industry

Fintech / Payments

Platform

Mobile product

Role

Product Designer

Year

2025

01 — Overview

Making digital payments feel immediate, familiar and secure.

Ewura Pay is shaped around everyday money movement. The interface prioritises clear balances, recognisable actions and straightforward transaction flows so users always understand what is happening.

02 — Challenge

Build confidence into every transaction.

Financial products need to communicate status, risk and next steps without adding friction. The challenge was balancing simplicity with enough context for users to feel informed and in control.

03 — Approach

Clarity before
complexity.

Core journeys were simplified around the most common actions. Confirmation states, transaction feedback and predictable navigation patterns reduce uncertainty throughout the experience.

04 — Product system

A consistent language for money and movement.

Reusable financial components, status patterns, typography and spacing create consistency across balances, transfers, activity and future payment features.

05 — Outcome

A payment experience designed around trust.

The final direction creates a cleaner, more coherent foundation for everyday payments while leaving room for the product to expand into a broader financial ecosystem.
